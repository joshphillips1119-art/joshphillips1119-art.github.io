Clip Autopilot

A personal tool that turns long videos into short, captioned vertical clips and posts them to its owner’s YouTube channel on a schedule.

What it does

  • Finds the strongest moments in long videos the owner adds: their own videos, or videos they have permission to use or that carry a Creative Commons licence.
  • Cuts each moment into a vertical Short with burned-in captions and a short on-screen hook.
  • Suggests a title, description and tags for each clip, which the owner reviews and approves.
  • Uploads approved clips to the owner’s YouTube channel at times the owner picks, and shows how each one performs.
  • Lists new comments on those Shorts and drafts replies, which are posted only when the owner sends them.

How it works

Clip Autopilot runs entirely on its owner’s own computer. There is no Clip Autopilot account, server or database: videos, settings and the YouTube sign-in stay on that computer. It connects to YouTube through Google’s sign-in and the YouTube Data API.

Your Google data

Clip Autopilot uses YouTube API Services. It uses YouTube access only to upload clips the owner has approved, manage those uploads, and read their statistics and comments. The privacy policy explains exactly what it accesses, where it is kept and who it is shared with. Use of the app is also covered by the terms of use.
