Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Clip Autopilot is a personal tool that runs on its owner’s own computer. It turns long videos into short vertical clips and posts them to the owner’s YouTube channel. It is not a hosted service: there is no Clip Autopilot server, account system, advertising or analytics.

YouTube API Services

Clip Autopilot uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

When you connect YouTube, Google asks you to let Clip Autopilot manage your YouTube account (the youtube and youtube.force-ssl permissions). It uses that access only to:

  • upload videos you have approved, with the title, description and tags you approved, and set their visibility and publish time;
  • add those uploads to a playlist you choose, and post a first comment you wrote, if you turn those options on;
  • read your channel’s name, picture, subscriber count and video count, and the names of your playlists;
  • read view, like and comment counts for videos it uploaded;
  • read comments on those videos, including commenters’ display names, and post your replies, each one only after you press Send.

It does not access any other Google data, such as Gmail, Drive, Calendar or contacts.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on the computer running Clip Autopilot. Your Google sign-in tokens are kept in a file only your user account can read. Videos, clip drafts, statistics and comments are kept in the app’s data folder. Nothing is sent to the app’s developer or to any server run for this app.

Who it is shared with

  • Google / YouTube, to carry out the actions listed above.
  • Anthropic, only for the AI features. Clip Autopilot uses Claude, through the Claude Code app on your computer or the Anthropic API, to choose clip moments and write titles from transcripts of the videos you add. If “Draft replies to new comments” is turned on, it also sends Claude the text and display names of new comments on your uploads, with the video’s title, so Claude can draft replies. Anthropic processes this under its own privacy policy. Whether it may be used to improve Anthropic’s models depends on the settings of your Claude account.

Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else. Clip Autopilot uses no cookies or third-party trackers.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until you delete it. Disconnect in the app’s Settings deletes the stored Google sign-in. Deleting the app’s data folder removes everything else. You can revoke Clip Autopilot’s access to your Google account at any time on Google’s third-party access page; it then cannot reach your YouTube account until you connect it again.

Children

Clip Autopilot is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
